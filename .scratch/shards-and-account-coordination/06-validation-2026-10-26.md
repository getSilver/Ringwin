Current account gates and their latches remain pinned through control-history retirement, so another account's recovery cannot remove a restriction and a shard can miss an entire intermediate recovery interval without retaining an unresolvable old gate.

Opening an account cause never clears a local latch or restores TradingAuthorization. Each shard must resolve and resume independently.

The existing supervisor qualification is shared by journal and durable Owner paths; heartbeat timeout and bounded Host ingress backpressure reach that qualified Owner path.
- The bounded, stream-bound `runtime.shard_inbox.Inbox` records overflow as a local durable gate. Failed enqueue leaves the rejected input with its caller; failed application retains the pending item. It is single-writer runtime state, not a durable economic queue or a new scheduler. Draining never resumes trading.
- `CoordinatorOwner.resolveVenueGate` admits only fully proved forced closure. It queries the immutable spool and hot or authenticated cold source history, requires completed allocation/ACK, delivery of a later complete account snapshot to all targets, a valid native account projection and a fresh healthy margin observation. Conflicting identity, unavailable history, pending delivery, incomplete forced sources, unknown facts and protocol faults cannot clear the gate. Margin resolution remains a separate explicit action; shard locks and authorization remain untouched.
